Why readability matters

Plain language helps people understand services and procedures the first time, reducing calls, errors and office visits.

Users cannot understand your content.

What we check

Standards for writing web content in plain language that people can read and understand quickly.

  • Reading level: Page text reads at or below a 7th grade level so most users can understand it.

Policy and guidance that supports plain language and readable web content. Guidance: 21st Century Integrated Digital Experience Act (IDEA), Automated Readability Index (ARI), Flesch–Kincaid readability tests, U.S. Federal Website Standards, Plain Writing Act of 2010.
