Most common failures by indicator

The check Washington public college sites fail most often in each indicator. Each indicator page lists the top five.

  • 28% (10 of 36 sites) fail this accessibility check: Elements with visible text labels have matching accessible names. A button or link's visible label matches what screen readers announce. More accessibility failures.
  • 53% (19 of 36 sites) fail this botability check: Link text. Links use descriptive text that tells users and search engines where they go. More botability failures.
  • 56% (20 of 36 sites) fail this security check: X-Content-Type-Options. The site tells browsers not to guess the type of files it serves. More security failures.
  • 94% (34 of 36 sites) fail this usability check: Open Graph image alt. The social media preview image has alt text for screen readers. More usability failures.
  • 19% (7 of 36 sites) fail this readability check: Reading level. Page text reads at or below a 7th grade level so most users can understand it. More readability failures.

About this data

ScanGov scans the homepage of each college and university site on our higher education list. Scores come from the checks in the ScanGov standards. Sites were scanned between 10/25/2026 and 11/8/2026. Each site's report shows its own scan date. A site counts as fully scanned when its homepage loaded and sent us its page content. Averages, grade shares, failure rates, and ranks use fully scanned sites only. We could not fully scan 6 sites. They did not respond, blocked the scanner, or sent no page content. They appear in the table without grades, with their scan status, and are left out of every figure. Grades follow the ScanGov grade scale. Rank follows the ScanGov ranking method. The full dataset, with every county's raw results, is in the ScanGov data repository.
