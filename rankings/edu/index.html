Most common failures by indicator

The check education sites fail most often in each indicator. Each indicator page lists the top five.

  • 32% (868 of 2,690 sites) fail this accessibility check: Heading elements appear in a sequentially-descending order. Page headings follow a logical order without skipping levels. More accessibility failures.
  • 44% (1,197 of 2,690 sites) fail this botability check: Link text. Links use descriptive text that tells users and search engines where they go. More botability failures.
  • 60% (1,617 of 2,690 sites) fail this security check: X-Content-Type-Options. The site tells browsers not to guess the type of files it serves. More security failures.
  • 90% (2,432 of 2,690 sites) fail this usability check: Open Graph image alt. The social media preview image has alt text for screen readers. More usability failures.
  • 41% (1,113 of 2,690 sites) fail this readability check: Reading level. Page text reads at or below a 7th grade level so most users can understand it. More readability failures.

About this data

ScanGov scans the homepage of each college and university site on our higher education list. Scores come from the checks in the ScanGov standards. Sites were scanned between 9/30/2026 and 11/8/2026. Each site's report shows its own scan date. A site counts as fully scanned when its homepage loaded and sent us its page content. Averages, grade shares, failure rates, and ranks use fully scanned sites only. We could not fully scan 304 sites. They did not respond, blocked the scanner, or sent no page content. They appear in the table without grades, with their scan status, and are left out of every figure. Grades follow the ScanGov grade scale. Rank follows the ScanGov ranking method. The full dataset, with every county's raw results, is in the ScanGov data repository.
