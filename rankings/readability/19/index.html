About readability

Write content at a reading level most users can understand.

Why it's important

Plain language helps people understand services and procedures the first time, reducing calls, errors and office visits.

Risks

  • Users cannot understand your content.

Guidance

What we use to grade readability:

  • 21st Century Integrated Digital Experience Act (IDEA)
  • Automated Readability Index (ARI)
  • Flesch–Kincaid readability tests
  • U.S. Federal Website Standards
  • Plain Writing Act of 2010
