What each check means

  • GovernmentOrganization Schema.org type. Homepage is marked up as an official government organization. Systems cannot verify you are an official government site.
  • Sitemap status. The sitemap file loads and returns a successful response. Search engines cannot find all your pages.
  • Sitemap XML. The sitemap file uses the required XML format. Search engines cannot read your sitemap.
  • Robots valid. The site has a robots.txt file that guides search engines. Search engines ignore your instructions.
  • Sitemap in robots.txt. The robots.txt file links to the sitemap. Search engines take longer to find your pages.

About botability

Help AI and search engines discover, crawl, and index your website.

Why it's important

Smart technology can better process and share public information, making services faster and more efficient for users.

Risks

  • AI tools cannot find or understand your content.

Guidance

What we use to grade botability:

  • 21st Century Integrated Digital Experience Act (IDEA)
  • The Web Robots Pages
  • Schema.org
  • sitemaps.org
  • Google Search Central

Standards

  • Crawlable
  • Content available in document
  • GovernmentOrganization Schema.org type
  • Sitemap status
  • Sitemap XML
  • Robots valid
  • Robots allowed
  • Sitemap in robots.txt
  • Canonical
  • Link text
  • hreflang

About this data

ScanGov scans the homepage of each county government site on our county list in Kansas. Scores come from the checks listed on standards.scangov.org. Sites were scanned between 7/18/2026 and 9/17/2026; each site's report shows its own scan date. A site counts as fully scanned when its homepage returned successfully and sent the scanner its page content. Averages, grade shares, failure rates, and ranks use fully scanned sites only. We weren't able to fully scan 13 sites because they did not respond, blocked the scanner, or returned no page content. They appear in the table without grades, each with its scan status, and are left out of every figure. Grades follow the ScanGov grade scale, and rank follows the ScanGov ranking method. The full dataset, including every county's raw results, is published in the ScanGov data repository.
