What each check means

  • Largest Contentful Paint (LCP). The page's main content loads within 2.5 seconds. Users wait too long and leave your page.
  • First Contentful Paint (FCP). The page starts displaying content within 1.8 seconds. Users see blank screen and think site is broken.
  • Page description. The page has a meta description that summarizes its content for search results. Search results do not show what your page does.
  • Open Graph image alt. The social media preview image has alt text for screen readers. Screen readers cannot describe shared images.
  • Interaction to Next Paint (INP). The page responds to user interactions in under 0.2 seconds. Site feels frozen when users click things.

About usability

Optimize content structure so users quickly find what they need.

Why it's important

Well-written pages help people understand laws, services, and procedures without confusion, reducing calls and office visits.

About this data

ScanGov scans the homepage of each county government site on our county list in New Jersey. Scores come from the checks listed on standards.scangov.org. Sites were scanned between 7/18/2026 and 9/17/2026; each site's report shows its own scan date. A site counts as responding when its homepage returned successfully. Averages, grade shares, and failure rates use responding sites only. 6 of the 18 responding sites did not return page content to the scanner, so content checks (text, Open Graph tags, readability) were not scored. They are included in every figure and marked "partial scan" in the table. Grades follow the ScanGov grade scale, and rank follows the ScanGov ranking method. The full dataset, including every county's raw results, is published in the ScanGov data repository.
