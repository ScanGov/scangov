About security

Secure your site and protect users with proper security practices.

Why it's important

Strong defenses prevent hackers from stealing personal information and other confidential data.

Risks

Guidance

What we use to grade security:

  • CISA Website Security
  • 21st Century Integrated Digital Experience Act (IDEA)
  • CISA Cybersecurity Performance Goals

About this data

ScanGov scans the homepage of each county government site on our county list in New Jersey. Scores come from the checks listed on standards.scangov.org. Sites were scanned between 7/18/2026 and 9/17/2026; each site's report shows its own scan date. A site counts as fully scanned when its homepage returned successfully and sent the scanner its page content. Averages, grade shares, failure rates, and ranks use fully scanned sites only. We weren't able to fully scan 7 sites because they did not respond, blocked the scanner, or returned no page content. They appear in the table without grades, each with its scan status, and are left out of every figure. Grades follow the ScanGov grade scale, and rank follows the ScanGov ranking method. The full dataset, including every county's raw results, is published in the ScanGov data repository.
