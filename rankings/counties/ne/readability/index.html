About readability

Write content at a reading level most users can understand.

Why it's important

Plain language helps people understand services and procedures the first time, reducing calls, errors and office visits.

Risks

  • Users cannot understand your content.

Guidance

What we use to grade readability:

  • 21st Century Integrated Digital Experience Act (IDEA)
  • Automated Readability Index (ARI)
  • Flesch–Kincaid readability tests
  • U.S. Federal Website Standards
  • Plain Writing Act of 2010

About this data

ScanGov scans the homepage of each county site on our county list in Nebraska. Scores come from the checks in the ScanGov standards. Sites were scanned between 10/24/2026 and 11/4/2026. Each site's report shows its own scan date. A site counts as fully scanned when its homepage loaded and sent us its page content. Averages, grade shares, failure rates, and ranks use fully scanned sites only. We could not fully scan 1 sites. They did not respond, blocked the scanner, or sent no page content. They appear in the table without grades, with their scan status, and are left out of every figure. Grades follow the ScanGov grade scale. Rank follows the ScanGov ranking method. The full dataset, with every county's raw results, is in the ScanGov data repository.
