About readability

Write content at a reading level most users can understand.

Why it's important

Plain language helps people understand services and procedures the first time, reducing calls, errors and office visits.

Risks

  • Users cannot understand your content.

Guidance

What we use to grade readability:

  • 21st Century Integrated Digital Experience Act (IDEA)
  • Automated Readability Index (ARI)
  • Flesch-Kincaid (FK)
  • U.S. Federal Website Standards
  • Plain Writing Act of 2010

About this data

ScanGov scans the homepage of each county government site on our county list in Georgia. Scores come from the checks listed on standards.scangov.org. Sites were scanned between 7/18/2026 and 10/2/2026; each site's report shows its own scan date. A site counts as fully scanned when its homepage returned successfully and sent the scanner its page content. Averages, grade shares, failure rates, and ranks use fully scanned sites only. We weren't able to fully scan 23 sites because they did not respond, blocked the scanner, or returned no page content. They appear in the table without grades, each with its scan status, and are left out of every figure. Grades follow the ScanGov grade scale, and rank follows the ScanGov ranking method. The full dataset, including every county's raw results, is published in the ScanGov data repository.
