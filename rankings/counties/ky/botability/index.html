What each check means

  • GovernmentOrganization Schema.org type. Homepage is marked up as an official government organization. Systems cannot verify you are an official government site.
  • Sitemap status. The sitemap file loads and returns a successful response. Search engines cannot find all your pages.
  • Sitemap XML. The sitemap file uses the required XML format. Search engines cannot read your sitemap.
  • Link text. Links use descriptive text that tells users and search engines where they go. Users cannot tell where links go.
  • Robots valid. The site has a robots.txt file that guides search engines. Search engines ignore your instructions.

About botability

Help AI and search engines discover, crawl, and index your website.

Why it's important

Smart technology can better process and share public information, making services faster and more efficient for users.

Risks

  • AI tools cannot find or understand your content.

Guidance

What we use to grade botability:

  • 21st Century Integrated Digital Experience Act (IDEA)
  • The Web Robots Pages
  • Schema.org
  • sitemaps.org
  • Google Search Central

Standards

  • Crawlable
  • Content available in document
  • GovernmentOrganization Schema.org type
  • Sitemap status
  • Sitemap XML
  • Robots valid
  • Robots allowed
  • Sitemap in robots.txt
  • Canonical
  • Link text
  • hreflang

About this data

ScanGov scans the homepage of each county government site on our county list in Kentucky. Scores come from the checks listed on standards.scangov.org. Sites were scanned between 7/18/2026 and 9/17/2026; each site's report shows its own scan date. A site counts as responding when its homepage returned successfully. Averages, grade shares, and failure rates use responding sites only. 6 of the 102 responding sites did not return page content to the scanner, so content checks (text, Open Graph tags, readability) were not scored. They are included in every figure and marked "partial scan" in the table. Grades follow the ScanGov grade scale, and rank follows the ScanGov ranking method. The full dataset, including every county's raw results, is published in the ScanGov data repository.
