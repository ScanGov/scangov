What each check means

  • GovernmentOrganization Schema.org type. Homepage is marked up as an official government organization. Systems cannot verify you are an official government site.
  • Sitemap in robots.txt. The robots.txt file links to the sitemap. Search engines take longer to find your pages.
  • Link text. Links use descriptive text that tells users and search engines where they go. Users cannot tell where links go.
  • Robots valid. The site has a robots.txt file that guides search engines. Search engines ignore your instructions.
  • Sitemap status. The sitemap file loads and returns a successful response. Search engines cannot find all your pages.

About botability

Help AI and search engines discover, crawl, and index your website.

Why it's important

Smart technology can better process and share public information, making services faster and more efficient for users.

Risks

  • AI tools cannot find or understand your content.

Guidance

What we use to grade botability:

  • 21st Century Integrated Digital Experience Act (IDEA)
  • The Web Robots Pages
  • Schema.org
  • sitemaps.org
  • Google Search Central

About this data

ScanGov scans the homepage of each county government site on our county list in South Dakota. Scores come from the checks listed on standards.scangov.org. Sites were scanned between 8/18/2026 and 9/18/2026; each site's report shows its own scan date. A site counts as responding when its homepage returned successfully. Averages, grade shares, and failure rates use responding sites only. 1 of the 52 responding sites did not return page content to the scanner, so content checks (text, Open Graph tags, readability) were not scored. They are included in every figure and marked "partial scan" in the table. Grades follow the ScanGov grade scale, and rank follows the ScanGov ranking method. The full dataset, including every county's raw results, is published in the ScanGov data repository.
