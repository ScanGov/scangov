What each check means

  • Open Graph image alt. The social media preview image has alt text for screen readers. Screen readers cannot describe shared images.
  • Open Graph description. The page has a description for social media previews. Shared links have no description on social media.
  • Open Graph site name. The site has a name for social media and browser display. Site name missing in social media shares.
  • Largest Contentful Paint (LCP). The page's main content loads within 2.5 seconds. Users wait too long and leave your page.
  • Page description. The page has a meta description that summarizes its content for search results. Search results do not show what your page does.

About usability

Optimize content structure so users quickly find what they need.

Why it's important

Well-written pages help people understand laws, services, and procedures without confusion, reducing calls and office visits.

Risks

  • Users cannot understand your information.

Guidance

What we use to grade usability:

  • 21st Century Integrated Digital Experience Act (IDEA)
  • U.S. Federal Website Standards
  • Automated Readability Index (ARI)
  • Flesch-Kincaid (FK)
  • Plain Writing Act of 2010
  • Google Search Central
  • Web Vitals / Core Web Vitals
  • Open Graph

About this data

ScanGov scans the homepage of each county government site on our county list in South Carolina. Scores come from the checks listed on standards.scangov.org. Sites were scanned between 7/18/2026 and 9/17/2026; each site's report shows its own scan date. A site counts as fully scanned when its homepage returned successfully and sent the scanner its page content. Averages, grade shares, failure rates, and ranks use fully scanned sites only. We weren't able to fully scan 4 sites because they did not respond, blocked the scanner, or returned no page content. They appear in the table without grades, each with its scan status, and are left out of every figure. Grades follow the ScanGov grade scale, and rank follows the ScanGov ranking method. The full dataset, including every county's raw results, is published in the ScanGov data repository.
