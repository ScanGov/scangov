What we scan

ScanGov scans the homepage of every federal site on our list, 866 sites. Each scan runs the checks on standards.scangov.org for accessibility, botability, security, usability, and readability. A site counts as fully scanned when its homepage responded and returned page content; sites that did not are listed with their scan status and left out of every figure here. The federal rankings list every site; Pulse for all sites compares the groups.

How sites are doing

Of 688 fully scanned sites, 15% (104) earn an A for overall score, 71% (488) a B or C, and 14% (96) a D or F. The average score is 79. The curve shows where scores cluster; the bar shows the share in each grade band.

Where sites fail

For each indicator, the five checks that fully scanned federal sites fail most often. A site can fail more than one check. In each chart, a check links to the list of federal sites failing it where we have one, or to its standard; the notes under the chart link every standard.

Accessibility

Meet WCAG standards so every type of user can use your website. The most common accessibility failure is Background and foreground colors have a sufficient contrast ratio: 28% (196 of 688 sites) fail it.

Botability

Help AI and search engines discover, crawl, and index your website. The most common botability failure is GovernmentOrganization Schema.org type: 93% (640 of 688 sites) fail it.

About this data

ScanGov scans the homepage of every federal site on our list. Scores come from the checks in the ScanGov standards. Sites were scanned between 9/30/2026 and 11/8/2026. Each site's report shows its own scan date. A site counts as fully scanned when its homepage loaded and sent us its page content. Averages, grade shares, failure rates, and ranks use fully scanned sites only. We could not fully scan 178 sites. They did not respond, blocked the scanner, or sent no page content. They appear in the table without grades, with their scan status, and are left out of every figure. Grades follow the ScanGov grade scale. Rank follows the ScanGov ranking method. The full dataset, with every county's raw results, is in the ScanGov data repository.
