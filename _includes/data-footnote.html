About this data

ScanGov scans the homepage of {{ footnoteSubject if footnoteSubject else "each county government site on our county list" }}{% if footnoteScope %} {{ footnoteScope }}{% endif %}. Scores come from the checks in the ScanGov standards. {% if footnoteScanWindow %}Sites were scanned between {{ footnoteScanWindow.from | standardFormatDate }} and {{ footnoteScanWindow.to | standardFormatDate }}. Each site's report shows its own scan date.{% else %}The last scan finished on {{ updates }}.{% endif %} A site counts as fully scanned when its homepage loaded and sent us its page content. Averages, grade shares, failure rates, and ranks use fully scanned sites only. {% if footnotePartialCount or footnoteNoResponseCount %}We could not fully scan {{ (footnotePartialCount + footnoteNoResponseCount) | numberFormat }} sites. They did not respond, blocked the scanner, or sent no page content. They appear in the table without grades, with their scan status, and are left out of every figure.{% endif %} Grades follow the ScanGov grade scale. Rank follows the ScanGov ranking method. The full dataset, with every county's raw results, is in the ScanGov data repository.
