How many websites does Project ScanGov scan?

Project ScanGov scans 7,046 U.S. websites: state, county, city, federal, and public college and university sites. 6,097 of them (87%) have a grade from the latest scan. Each scan checks the homepage for accessibility, botability, security, usability, and readability.

How we scan

Project ScanGov, a ScanGov project, loads the homepage of each site on the list and checks it against the standards for accessibility, botability, security, usability, and readability. It does not crawl the rest of the site. ScanGov itself scans every page of your own site.

  • Accessibility: 66 standards
  • Botability: 11 standards
  • Security: 9 standards
  • Usability: 16 standards
  • Readability: 1 standards

The scanner identifies itself as ScanGovBot. A site that blocks it stays on the list without a grade. The scan documentation explains how scans run and what each check means.

Frequently asked questions

Does Project ScanGov scan every page of a site?

No. The scores on this site come from each site's homepage. ScanGov, the service, scans every page of a site you add to it.

Which sites are on the list?

State, county, city, federal, and public college and university websites: 7,046 in all. The rankings list every one.

When were the sites last scanned?

The latest scan was on 11/8/2026. Each site's report shows its own scan date.

Why does my site have no grade?

A site gets a grade when its homepage loaded and sent us its page content. A site that did not respond, blocked the scanner, or sent no page content stays on the list without a grade, and its status says why. Find the code on the status page, and allow ScanGovBot if your firewall turns it away.

How do I add a site?

Use the contact form to tell us about a site that belongs on the list.
